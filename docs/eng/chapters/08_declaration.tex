\textit{How the code was created:} At the start, I planned the structure of the
pipeline myself: loading and normalizing the data, splits, frozen backbone with
regression head, training and evaluation. In the first days, ChatGPT helped me
to turn this structure into Python code. I ran, tested and adapted the code
myself. The technical decisions, i.e.\ model, splits, error measure and
experiments, were mine.

The topic, the research questions, the design of experiments E0 to E3, the
recording of the webcam data and all training runs and demo tests are my own
work. The additional evaluations E4 (smoothing, suggested by my supervisor) and
E5 (second person) were designed together with Claude and run by me. I checked
all results of the AI tools critically and corrected them where necessary;
the numbers in Chapter~4 were recomputed from the stored result files. The
technical statements are based on the cited literature; AI tools are not used
as sources. I take full responsibility for the entire content of this thesis.
